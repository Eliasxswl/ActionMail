# Active v2 annotation and acceptance policy

This is the current policy for the active 60-case registry, not a rewrite of frozen v1.5 labels. The old single-action policy and its versions are archived under `backup/v1.*/docs/`. Manifests, historical replies and original scores remain immutable.

The unit is a newest email for a named target recipient. Output is action, no_action or needs_review, with one reason and supporting original evidence for every status when text exists. Historical requests alone do not renew work. Shared recipients need not imply exclusive ownership. Information, completed work, optional suggestions for consideration and another person's plans do not create a target task. A concrete request to assess, decide or answer can create its narrower next step without accepting recommendations.

Group steps for the same deliverable. Preserve independently completable tasks or different deadlines; maximum three. More tasks require review. Identifying a task does not require every execution input or approval. Explicit missing document dependencies, ownership conflict, empty newest body or unavailable decisive content can require review.

Read external task details and primary-content pointers; exclude unrelated material. Cited external text must actually have been supplied. Quotes must match original registered sources, including distinct historical header IDs. Safe whitespace/known soft-wrap normalization may recover an exact original span; fuzzy similarity cannot approve changed facts. A failed model contract may be corrected once per email and strictly revalidated. Valid abstention and transport/read/budget failures do not trigger retries.

Use ISO dates only with explicit resolvable information; preserve null when received-time anchor or timezone is absent. Do not infer a task deadline from a meeting date alone.

The frozen base 50 retains v1.5 gold byte-for-byte. V2 amendments are separately hash-bound: A16 permits two actions; C11 accepts either conservative review or a single answer-question interpretation. Supplement references were owner-approved. Scores for status/count/source/read/coverage remain distinct from human meaning/completeness/deadline judgments. Do not revise gold after observing a failure without explicit adjudication and preserved historical records. The 60 cases are a development/regression suite, not an untouched held-out sample.
