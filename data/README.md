# Original public evaluation inputs

This minimal corpus contains original public raw threads and the selected Enron parent export/text PDF required by the 60-case registry and negative tests. Unused downloads are in the verified history ZIP. These files are not the owner's personal inbox.

Source hashes, target recipients and authorship are recorded in `ActionMail/evaluation/cases.jsonl` and `supplement_v2_approved.jsonl`. Public MailEx/Enron-derived cases and authored MIME fixtures have distinct origins. Authored fixtures stay with evaluation or tests. See [data and evaluation](../ActionMail/docs/evaluation.md) and [experimental protocol](../experiments/PROTOCOL.md).

Experiments independently retain exactly their supplied inputs under the hash-bound `experiments/data/frozen-core/`. Original-corpus and experimental-bundle validation are separate; neither rewrites sources. Relative dates without trustworthy received anchors remain unresolved.
